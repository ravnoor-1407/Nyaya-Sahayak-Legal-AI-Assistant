ABSTRACT & PROBLEM FORMULATION:

In this laboratory assignment, we investigate the empirical convergence rates of sparse vector indexing in multidimensional vector spaces. We compare dense embedding representations generated by BERT architectures against traditional BM25 inverted indexing mechanisms for text search.

METHODOLOGY & EXPERIMENTAL SETUP:

1. Preprocessing: Tokenization using Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) with vocabulary size V = 32,000.

2. Similarity metric: Cosine similarity computed over L2-normalized 768-dimensional tensors.

3. Hardware: Nvidia RTX 4090 GPU, PyTorch 2.4, CUDA 12.1.

RESULTS & OBSERVATIONS:

The experimental ablation indicates that reciprocal rank fusion (RRF) achieves a Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR@10) of 0.892, outperforming standalone semantic search by 14.3% across multi-class document clustering.

CONCLUSION: Hybrid retrieval strategies consistently yield optimal balance between precision and computational latency in production deep learning pipelines.
