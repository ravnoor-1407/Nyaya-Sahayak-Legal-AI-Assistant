Essential Citizen Legal Protections

1. Police Notice vs Arrest (Section 35 BNSS / Arnesh Kumar)

For offences with punishment up to 7 years (theft, cheating, matrimonial disputes):

  • Police CANNOT mechanically arrest. They must first issue a written Notice of Appearance under Section 35(3) BNSS.
  • As long as you comply and appear, police cannot arrest you without prior written reasons and Judicial Magistrate permission.

2. Fundamental Arrestee Safeguards (D.K. Basu Guidelines)

Mandatory constitutional rights under Articles 21 & 22 and Section 47 BNSS:

  • Arrest memo must be signed by at least one independent family member or witness.
  • Right to have a family member informed of detention within 8 to 12 hours.
  • Must be produced before the nearest Judicial Magistrate within 24 hours.

3. First-Time Undertrial Bail (Section 479 BNSS)

Statutory mandatory bail right under the new 2023 criminal law:

  • First-time offenders (never convicted previously) must be released on mandatory bail upon serving one-third (1/3rd) of the maximum sentence.
  • Superintendent of Jail must automatically apply to the Court on behalf of the prisoner.

4. Online Bank Fraud Golden Hour (Call 1930)

Immediate steps if money was transferred via UPI, net banking, or debit card fraud:

  • Call 1930 immediately within the first 2 hours. Keep the 12-digit UTR number and recipient bank account ready.
  • Alert your bank nodal branch within 3 days for zero-liability protection under RBI guidelines.
