ABSTRACT:
Autonomous quadcopter navigation in indoor obstacle-dense environments poses substantial control challenges. In this project, we formulate the path-planning objective as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) and implement Deep Q-Networks (DQN) with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). The simulation results demonstrate a 94.2% obstacle-avoidance success rate across 500 stochastic maze configurations.
