(Or copy text from: 1_Police_Summons_Notice_Sec35_BNSS.txt)

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
- TRAFFIC LIGHT STATUS: RED DANGER BADGE
  "DANGER / HIGH RISK — URGENT ACTION REQUIRED"
- DETECTED DEADLINE:
  "Appearance ordered within 48 Hours before Investigating Officer at Cyber Crime Police Station"
- IDENTIFIED SECTIONS:
  BNSS Section 35(3), BNS Section 318(4), IT Act Section 66D
- PLAIN LANGUAGE BREAKDOWN:
  1. This is a formal criminal summons under new procedural law.
  2. Failure to comply can result in non-bailable arrest warrant under Section 35(6) BNSS.
  3. You have the right to consult legal counsel under Article 22(1).
