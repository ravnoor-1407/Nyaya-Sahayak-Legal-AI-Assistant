RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (EXTRACT)

TENANCY DISPUTE & FORFEITURE CLAUSES

THIS RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT is executed on this 1st day of August 2026 between Mr. Rameshwar Dayal (Landlord) and Ms. Sunita Mehra (Tenant).

Clause 4 (Monthly Rent & Maintenance):
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 35,000/- on or before the 5th day of each English calendar month.

Clause 6 (Refundable Security Deposit):
The Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,05,000/- (Three Months Rent) with the Landlord, refundable upon handover of vacant physical possession.

Clause 8 (Lock-in Period & Deposit Forfeiture):
The tenancy is subject to a strict mandatory lock-in period of 24 months. If the Tenant vacates or terminates the lease before 24 months for any reason whatsoever, the entire security deposit of Rs. 1,05,000/- shall be forfeited by the Landlord without any right of set-off or refund. Furthermore, the Landlord shall have the immediate right to disconnect electricity and water amenities without seeking any court or rent tribunal order.

Clause 14 (Dispute Resolution):
Any disputes arising shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of civil courts at Gurgaon, Haryana.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this extract.
