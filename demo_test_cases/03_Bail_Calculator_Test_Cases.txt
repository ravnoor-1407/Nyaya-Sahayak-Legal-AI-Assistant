================================================================================
         03 — SECTION 479 BNSS BAIL CALCULATOR TEST CASES (/bail-calculator)
================================================================================

BACKGROUND:
Section 479 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) replaced old Section 436A CrPC.
It introduced a landmark reform for undertrials:
1. First-time offenders: Mandatory release on bail after serving 1/3rd of the maximum sentence.
2. Other offenders: Mandatory release on bail after serving 1/2 of the maximum sentence.
3. Excluded: Offences punishable by death or life imprisonment.

HOW TO TEST:
1. Open: https://nyaya-sahayak-legal-ai-assistant.vercel.app/bail-calculator
2. Input the values from any of the scenarios below.
3. Click "Calculate Undertrial Bail Entitlement".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 1: FIRST-TIME OFFENDER ENTITLED UNDER 1/3RD RULE (SUCCESS / ELIGIBLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUTS:
- 1. Select Alleged Offence: "Theft - Section 303(2) BNS" (Max: 3 Years / 36 Months)
- 2. Is the Undertrial a First-Time Offender?: YES (First-Timer)
- 3. Date of First Police Remand / Judicial Custody: Set date to 14 MONTHS AGO 
     (e.g., if today is November 2026, set to September 2025).

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
- Maximum Sentence: 36 Months
- Mandatory Threshold: 12 Months (1/3rd)
- Time Served: ~14 Months
- VERDICT BANNER: 
  "MANDATORY BAIL APPLICABLE — IMMEDIATE RELEASE ENTITLEMENT" (Green Banner)
- PREVIEW BAIL APPLICATION:
  Pre-populates an application under Section 479 BNSS addressed to the Chief Judicial Magistrate / Sessions Judge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 2: REPEAT OFFENDER ENTITLED UNDER 1/2 RULE (SUCCESS / ELIGIBLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUTS:
- 1. Select Alleged Offence: "Cheating & Dishonestly Inducing Delivery - Section 318(4) BNS" (Max: 7 Years / 84 Months)
- 2. Is the Undertrial a First-Time Offender?: NO (Previous Conviction or Repeat Undertrial)
- 3. Date of First Police Remand / Judicial Custody: Set date to 45 MONTHS AGO
     (e.g. February 2023).

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
- Maximum Sentence: 84 Months
- Mandatory Threshold: 42 Months (1/2)
- Time Served: ~45 Months
- VERDICT BANNER:
  "MANDATORY BAIL APPLICABLE — IMMEDIATE RELEASE ENTITLEMENT" (Green Banner)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 3: CAPITAL OFFENCE / LIFE IMPRISONMENT (EXCLUDED BY STATUTE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUTS:
- 1. Select Alleged Offence: "Murder - Section 103(1) BNS" (Penalty: Death / Life Imprisonment)
- 2. Is the Undertrial a First-Time Offender?: YES
- 3. Date of Custody: Any date (e.g. 5 years ago)

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
- VERDICT:
  "NOT APPLICABLE — STATUTORY EXCLUSION" (Red Banner)
- STATUTORY REASON:
  Section 479(1) of BNSS explicitly excludes offences punishable with death or life imprisonment. 
  Bail can only be sought under regular discretionary grounds under Section 480 or 483 BNSS.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 4: PENDING THRESHOLD (NOT YET ELIGIBLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUTS:
- 1. Select Alleged Offence: "Criminal Breach of Trust - Section 316 BNS" (Max: 5 Years / 60 Months)
- 2. Is the Undertrial a First-Time Offender?: YES (Threshold: 20 Months)
- 3. Date of Custody: Set date to 8 MONTHS AGO.

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
- Time Served: 8 Months
- Required: 20 Months
- VERDICT:
  "MANDATORY BAIL NOT YET REACHED" (Amber Banner)
- COUNTDOWN: "12 Months remaining until mandatory bail eligibility under Section 479 BNSS."
