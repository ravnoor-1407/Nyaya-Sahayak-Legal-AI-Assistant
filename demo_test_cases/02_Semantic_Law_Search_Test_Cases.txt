TEST CASE 2.1: EXACT PROVISION LOOKUP (BNSS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUERY:
Section 105 BNSS

EXPECTED TOP RESULT:
BNSS Section 105 — "Recording of search and seizure through audio-video electronic means."
KEY TEXT: Mandates audio-video electronic recording of search of place or seizure of property.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUERY:
Murder definition and punishment

EXPECTED TOP RESULT:
BNS Section 101 (Murder definition) & BNS Section 103 (Punishment for murder).
NOTE: Proves the search maps the old Section 302 IPC concept directly to the new 2023 code.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BSA Section 63 — "Admissibility of electronic records."
